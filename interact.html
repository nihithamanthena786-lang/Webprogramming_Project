01. Fry the onions

Thinly slice the onions and heat oil in a deep pan. Fry them in batches over medium heat until golden brown and crispy. Remove and drain the excess oil. Keep some fried onions aside for layering.

05:00

02. Marinate the chicken

In a large bowl, combine chicken pieces with thick curd, ginger-garlic paste, red chilli powder, turmeric, biryani masala, lemon juice and salt. Add mint and coriander leaves. Mix well and coat every piece. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour.

30:00

03. Prepare the rice

Take long-grain basmati rice and wash it gently two to three times until the water becomes clearer. Drain the water and soak the rice in fresh water for about 30 minutes. This helps the grains cook evenly.

10:00

04. Parboil the rice

Bring plenty of water to a boil with salt, bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves and cardamom. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is approximately 70% done. The grains should still have a slight bite. Drain immediately.

08:00

05. Layer the biryani

Take a heavy-bottomed handi and spread the marinated chicken evenly at the bottom. Add a layer of partially cooked rice on top. Sprinkle fried onions, mint leaves and coriander leaves. Add another layer of rice and repeat the toppings.

05:00

06. Add saffron and cook on dum

Pour warm saffron-infused milk over the rice. Add melted ghee and a little reserved fried onion. Cover the handi tightly with a lid and seal the edges with dough. Place it on a flat pan over low heat and cook for approximately 30 minutes.

30:00

07. Rest and serve!

Turn off the heat and allow the biryani to rest for 10 minutes before opening the handi. Gently fluff the rice from the sides without breaking the grains. Serve hot with raita, salan and freshly sliced onions.

10:00
